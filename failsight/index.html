1 · Add photos of the failed part

Add up to 8 photos, or a video to grab frames from. More angles = better diagnosis. Mark each photo where the damage started.

Best photo set: ① Overview: whole part in the machine  ② Close-up of damage  ③ Fracture face / burnt area (straight-on, good light)  ④ Mating part (bearing, shaft, connector, valve body)  ⑤ Nameplate / label (model, voltage, rating)  ⑥ Surroundings (dirt, oil, water, cable route). Tip: put a coin or ruler beside the part for size.

🧠 Why-Why analysis (AI-assisted)

Gemini AI builds a 5-Why chain from your photos, video frames, parts & functions, troubleshooter answers, and the diagnosis. Each why shows the evidence and how to confirm it. Mark each one Confirmed or Rejected, correct it, then update.
